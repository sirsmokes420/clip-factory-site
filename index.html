Clip Factory Uploader

Clip Factory Uploader is a personal tool I use to upload my own videos to my own TikTok accounts as drafts.

It runs only on my own computer. It sends a video I made to the inbox of a TikTok account I own, where I review it and decide in the TikTok app whether to post it. It never posts anything publicly by itself.

It is not a product or a service, and it is not offered to anyone else.
