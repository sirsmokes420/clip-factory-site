Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Whose data

Clip Factory Uploader is used only by me, with TikTok accounts I own. It collects no data from anyone else.

What it stores

When I authorise one of my TikTok accounts, TikTok returns login tokens (an access token, a refresh token and the account's ID). These are stored only on my own computer, in its encrypted system keychain. They are not stored on any server or on this website.

What it sends

It sends the videos I choose to upload, and the tokens needed to upload them, directly to TikTok. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Sharing

Nothing is shared, sold or given to anyone. This website has no analytics, cookies, forms or tracking.

Removing access

I can revoke the tool's access at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) and delete the stored tokens from my computer.
